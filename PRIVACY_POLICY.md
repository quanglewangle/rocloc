# Privacy Policy for RocLoc

**Last updated: June 2026**

## Overview

RocLoc is a callsign lookup and radio planning tool for UK amateur radio operators. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

## Data collected and used

### Location data
The app accesses your device's GPS location when you use the **Here** tab. This is used solely to centre the map on your position and calculate line-of-sight to nearby sites. Your location is never stored on our servers, never transmitted to third parties, and is not retained by the app beyond the current session.

### Callsign lookups
When you search for a callsign, the app contacts the QRZ.com XML database via a server at fimblefowl.co.uk. The callsign you enter is sent to that server to perform the lookup. Results are cached locally on your device using an on-device database to allow offline access to previously searched callsigns. No personal data about you (the user) is sent to QRZ.com — only the callsign being looked up.

### Terrain data
If you use the offline terrain feature, terrain tile files are downloaded from fimblefowl.co.uk and stored on your device. These are geographic elevation files only and contain no personal data.

### Sites and pins
Sites and pins displayed in the app are fetched from a server at fimblefowl.co.uk. This is amateur radio station data (callsigns, locations, antenna heights) submitted by operators voluntarily. No data about you or your device is sent when fetching this data.

## Data we do not collect

- We do not collect your name, email address, or any account information
- We do not use advertising identifiers or analytics
- We do not track usage or behaviour
- We do not share any data with third parties

## Third-party services

- **QRZ.com** — callsign data source. Subject to QRZ's own privacy policy at qrz.com
- **OpenStreetMap** — map tiles. Subject to OpenStreetMap's privacy policy at openstreetmap.org/privacy
- **fimblefowl.co.uk** — lookup relay and terrain tile server, operated by the app developer

## Data storage

All cached data (callsigns, terrain tiles) is stored locally on your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the app.
